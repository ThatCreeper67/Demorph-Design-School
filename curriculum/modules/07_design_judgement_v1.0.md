# Module 7 — Design Judgement

Version: 1.0.0  
Curriculum source: `design_curriculum.md` v1.0, Level 5 — Design critique.

## Learning goal

Evaluate communication clarity, audience suitability, consistency, readability, composition, hierarchy, aesthetic coherence, and alignment with the supplied purpose.

## Judgement rules

- A purpose-dependent judgment requires a brief or clearly stated assumption.
- Separate objective measurements, brief-dependent judgments, and subjective judgments.
- Avoid unexplained labels such as “professional” or “good.”
- Keep dimensions separate; do not collapse them into one unexplained score.
- Human-reviewed ordinal ratings are judgments, not universal ground truth.

## Assessment

Use dimension-specific ratings, reviewer agreement, evidence-groundedness, and unsupported-claim rate. Preserve the reference, prediction, method, and explanation for each score.
