# Module 8 — Design Critique

Version: 1.0.0  
Curriculum source: `design_curriculum.md` v1.0, Levels 5–6 — Design critique; creative reasoning and revision.

## Learning goal

Explain what works, what needs attention, why, which elements or relationships are involved, and what targeted revision could help. State expected impact, trade-offs, severity, and uncertainty.

## Critique record

Each claim links to element IDs or image regions, cites visual evidence, marks whether it is objective, brief-dependent, or subjective, and records uncertainty or an alternative reading where relevant.

## Recommendation rules

- Tie every recommendation to a diagnosed issue and evidence.
- Prefer a specific, feasible change that preserves successful parts of the design.
- Explain expected benefit and trade-off; do not present taste as fact.
- Never manufacture preference labels. Preferred/rejected alternatives require human review and a recorded reason.

## Assessment

Human review should score correctness, evidence quality, usefulness, constraint preservation, and trade-offs separately. Evaluate recommendations on held-out designs before accepting them.
