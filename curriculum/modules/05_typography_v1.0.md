# Module 5 — Typography

Version: 1.0.0  
Curriculum source: `design_curriculum.md` v1.0, Level 4 — Design principles and composition.

## Learning goal

Analyze type roles, broad classification and personality, pairing, scale, weight, tracking, leading, line length, wrapping, legibility, readability, contrast, and typographic hierarchy.

## Reasoning rules

- Describe visible letterform properties; do not invent exact font identities from resemblance.
- Distinguish legibility of individual forms from readability of text at its size and context.
- Do not claim copy is readable when it cannot be reliably transcribed.
- Consider type/background contrast, line length, spacing, and neighboring elements.
- Treat type classification and personality as uncertain interpretations when evidence is limited.

## Assessment

Evaluate hierarchy and readability with reviewed labels; use OCR only where transcription is verified. Record evidence and uncertainty for each judgment.
